Create a premium, warm, senior-friendly Korean mobile app UI flow for an AI family autobiography service called “DEARLOG”.

DEARLOG is a voice-based family memory archiving service. It helps parents record their life stories through phone-call style AI interviews, and helps children collect questions, upload family photos, review progress, check chapters, and help complete a family autobiography. When the interview progress reaches 100%, the app generates an autobiography PDF/book and a source-grounded family persona chatbot.

The design should feel warm, calm, premium, trustworthy, emotional, and family-oriented. It should feel like a high-quality digital family album and autobiography archive, not a cold AI chatbot. Use Korean typography, large readable text, rounded cards, soft shadows, elegant spacing, family photo album visuals, book cover motifs, voice recording elements, and calm editorial layouts.

Color palette:
- Background / Parchment: #FAF3E8
- Surface / Card: #FFFDF8
- Primary Brown: #8B6A3E
- Deep Ink Brown: #3D2C1E
- Amber Clay: #C8956C
- Sage: #6B8F71
- Warm Gray: #E8E0D4
- Sepia Tint: #F2D9B8
- Text Primary: #3D2C1E
- Text Secondary: #7A6A5C
- Border: #E8E0D4

Important design direction:
- Parent mode must be extremely simple and accessible for older users.
- Child mode should look more premium, detailed, and management-oriented.
- Include a font size adjustment feature in parent mode.
- Show both the normal text UI version and the large text UI version.
- The large text UI version should use bigger typography, fewer elements per screen, larger buttons, and clearer contrast.
- Use warm brown as the main CTA color.
- Use sage for success, saved status, and trust indicators.
- Use sepia or amber accents for memory cards and family archive elements.
- Avoid cold blue AI styling, futuristic neon, complicated dashboards, tiny text, and childish illustrations.
