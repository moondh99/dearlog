Create a warm, premium Korean mobile app UI flow for “DEARLOG”, a family autobiography and memory archive service.

DEARLOG helps families preserve parents’ life stories through gentle phone-based conversations. Parents answer questions by voice, and children can add questions, upload family photos, check progress, review chapters, verify original voice records, invite family members, and complete a family autobiography. When the story collection is complete, the app creates an autobiography PDF/book and a family memory conversation space based on the recorded stories.

Important tone:
Do not make this feel like an AI chatbot app or a futuristic tech product. Make it feel like a warm digital family album, a premium autobiography service, and a quiet archive of family memories. The technology should feel invisible and supportive. Avoid using “AI” visually or textually unless absolutely necessary. Focus on words like “기록”, “목소리”, “원문”, “가족”, “자서전”, “기억”, “챕터”, “대화”.

Design mood:
- warm
- calm
- premium
- trustworthy
- family-centered
- editorial

- book-like
- emotionally restrained
- not childish
- not futuristic
- not dashboard-heavy

Visual language:
Use warm book and album references: hardcover book, family photo frame, album page, handwritten note, bookmark, chapter card, paper texture, voice record card, calm progress marker, archival stamp. Use elegant rounded cards, soft shadows, generous spacing, large Korean typography, and refined icons. Avoid robot icons, glowing gradients, blue tech colors, chat bubble overload, futuristic patterns, and generic AI assistant visuals.
